# Job Hunt — Setup Guide

## How to get this on your phone AND laptop (synced)

### Step 1 — Get a free GitHub account
1. Go to github.com → click "Sign up"
2. Choose a username, add your email, create a password
3. Verify your email

### Step 2 — Create a new repository
1. Click the "+" icon top right → "New repository"
2. Name it: `job-hunt`
3. Set it to **Public**
4. Click "Create repository"

### Step 3 — Upload your files
1. On your new repo page, click "uploading an existing file"
2. Drag and drop ALL 4 files:
   - index.html
   - manifest.json
   - sw.js
   - icon.svg
3. Click "Commit changes"

### Step 4 — Turn on GitHub Pages
1. Go to Settings → Pages (left sidebar)
2. Under "Source" → select "Deploy from a branch"
3. Branch: main → folder: / (root) → Save
4. Wait ~60 seconds
5. Your URL will be: https://YOURUSERNAME.github.io/job-hunt

### Step 5 — Add to iPhone home screen
1. Open that URL in **Safari** (must be Safari, not Chrome)
2. Tap the Share button (box with arrow pointing up) at the bottom
3. Scroll down → tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add"
5. Done — it's on your home screen like an app

### Step 6 — Open on laptop
Just go to https://YOURUSERNAME.github.io/job-hunt in any browser.
Bookmark it.

## Sync: how it works

Your checkmarks and edits save in your browser's local storage on each device.

To fully sync phone ↔ laptop:
- Use the **Export** button (tap the menu icon) → copies your data as text
- Paste into the **Import** field on your other device
- This manually syncs everything

For automatic sync in the future, we can upgrade to use Google Drive storage (just ask).
